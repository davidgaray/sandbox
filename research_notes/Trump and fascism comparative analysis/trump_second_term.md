This was compliance with the ruling paired with a workaround. **[verified]** — [Yahoo/AP-syndicated](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/trump-says-hes-raising-global-165626632.html); [WVTM13/Hearst](https://www.wvtm13.com/article/how-trump-is-imposing-new-tariffs-supreme-courts/70450669)

Trump as moot**, because the government had deported all three named plaintiffs under the INA rather than the AEA. That left the AEA's legality unresolved and presumably vacated the panel opinion. **[verified]** — [Volokh/Reason](https://reason.com/volokh/2026/08/13/en-banc-fifth-circuit-dismisses-alien-enemies-act-case-as-moot/); [Steve Vladeck](https://www.stevevladeck.com/p/244-the-end-of-the-alien-enemies); [A.R.P. v.
